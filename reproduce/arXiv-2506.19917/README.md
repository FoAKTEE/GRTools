# arXiv:2506.19917 — Fairbairn & Dittmann, "Pushing the limits of eccentricity in planet-disc interactions"

MNRAS (2025) 565–586, doi:10.1093/mnras/staf1399; reproduced at **v3**.  Third Newtonian paper of the
EMRI-ECC mission: the FR22 + FR24 linear pipeline pushed to m ≤ 170–220, |l − m| ≤ 40–80 and e ≤ 0.7,
validated against 2D locally isothermal Disco hydrodynamics for (p, q) ∈ {(1.5, 1.0), (1.5, 0.0),
(0.5, 0.0)}, h_p = 0.06.  It supplies the high-eccentricity Newtonian targets that the relativistic
eccentric extension must recover in the p/M → ∞ limit sequence.

## Reference data (measurements of the published figures — never reproduced results)

Simulation (Disco) content is **digitised reference only** (mission default: the hydro runs are not
re-run).  The references live in the emri-disk mission repo:

- `results/emri-eccentric/paper_EMRI-ECC/evidence/fd25-vector-digitise/` (`ECC::fd25-vector-digitise`) —
  every series of the 14 vector PDFs (Figs 4–13, A1–A4) in paper units, axes calibrated on the tick
  marks (worst tick refit 2.4e-3 pt), with per-panel calibration residuals.  Against the author data repo,
  the digitised LINEAR curves deviate by a median 0.001 pt (worst 0.24 pt, at a dT/dr spike).  The one
  systematic exception is Fig 10 at e = 0.12: the plotted F_J lies 0.009 F_J,0 below `amf.out` inside
  r ≈ 1.1, which is a data difference (not attributed) and not a calibration effect.  The digitiser
  also recovers the **27-point e-grid** of Figs 7–9: 0, 0.01–0.06
  step 0.01, 0.065–0.1 step 0.005, 0.11, 0.12, 0.15–0.30 step 0.03, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7 (max deviation from
  the 0.005 lattice 8.8e-6).  The Disco stars sit at 0.01, 0.06, e₃, 0.12, 0.18, 0.24, 0.30, 0.4, 0.5,
  0.6, 0.7, where e₃ = 0.0832, 0.0839 and 0.0722 for (1.5,1.0), (1.5,0.0) and (0.5,0.0).
- `results/emri-eccentric/paper_EMRI-ECC/evidence/fd25-raster-reference/` (`ECC::fd25-raster-reference`) —
  per-panel crops of the PNG maps (Figs 1–3).  They are regenerated by `raster_reference.py`, which checks
  each crop's sha256 against its manifest.  The script also measures the colour limits, ±2.7944 (Fig 1),
  ±5.4350 (Fig 2) and ±5.4225 (Fig 3), and decides the Fig 3 sign from the pixels: **Disco − Linear**.
  A lossy colour-bar inversion is feasible, to ~1 colour band; values beyond the limits are saturated.
