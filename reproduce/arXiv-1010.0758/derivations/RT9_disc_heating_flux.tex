\section*{Disc-heating emergent flux, no-viscosity limit (1010.0758 RT9)}

For an inviscid disc ($W_\phi{}^r=0$), the angular-momentum (\texttt{eq:cL}) and energy (\texttt{eq:cE})
conservation laws — with rest-mass conservation (\texttt{eq:cM}) used to eliminate $\dot\Sigma$ — reduce to a
$2\times2$ linear system for the radial drift $u^r$ and the emergent flux $F$:
\begin{equation}
  \begin{pmatrix} r\mathcal{L}'\Sigma & 2r\mathcal{L}\\[2pt] r\mathcal{E}'\Sigma & 2r\mathcal{E}\end{pmatrix}
  \begin{pmatrix} u^r\\ F\end{pmatrix}
  = \frac{1}{2\pi}\frac{\rmd T}{\rmd r}\begin{pmatrix} 1\\ \Omega_{\rm s}\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Cramer's rule gives (\texttt{eq:flux-diss}, \texttt{eq:FluxReq})
\begin{equation}
  u^r=\frac{1}{2\pi r\Sigma}\frac{\rmd T}{\rmd r}\,\frac{\mathcal{E}-\Omega_{\rm s}\mathcal{L}}
       {\mathcal{E}\mathcal{L}'-\mathcal{L}\mathcal{E}'},
  \qquad
  F=\frac{1}{4\pi r}\frac{\rmd T}{\rmd r}\,\frac{\Omega_{\rm s}\mathcal{L}'-\mathcal{E}'}
       {\mathcal{E}\mathcal{L}'-\mathcal{L}\mathcal{E}'} .
\end{equation}
The denominator simplifies via the circular-orbit identity $\mathcal{E}'=\Omega\mathcal{L}'$ (so
$\mathcal{E}\mathcal{L}'-\mathcal{L}\mathcal{E}'=\mathcal{L}'(\mathcal{E}-\Omega\mathcal{L})$).

\paragraph{Verification.}
(a) $\mathcal{E}'=\Omega\mathcal{L}'$ for Schwarzschild ($\mathcal{E}'=(r-6)/[2r^{3/2}(r-3)^{3/2}]$,
$\mathcal{L}'=(r-6)/[2(r-3)^{3/2}]$, $\Omega=r^{-3/2}$): $\max|\mathcal{E}'-\Omega\mathcal{L}'|=8.7\times10^{-19}$.
(b) Solving the $2\times2$ system numerically for random $(\mathcal{E},\mathcal{L},\mathcal{L}',\mathcal{E}',
\Omega_{\rm s},\Sigma,\rmd T/\rmd r)$ reproduces the closed forms for $u^r,F$ to $5.6\times10^{-12}$ (2000
trials). \textbf{[SOLID]} (data computed by the Julia port).
