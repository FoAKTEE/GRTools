\end{equation}
Substituting the epicyclic identity $\kappa(R)=\sqrt{C_{020}C_{002}}$ (\texttt{eq:kappaR}) and the

  \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
  T^{(m)} = \pm\rmi m\,\frac{1-\rme^{-\rmi D(R)\Delta t}}{2D(R)}\sqrt{\tfrac{C_{020}}{C_{002}}}\,

\end{equation}

\end{equation}
The leading $\pm$ is the resonance sign ($D=m(\Omega-\Omega_{\rm s})\mp\kappa$); it matches the internal
